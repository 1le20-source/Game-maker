# Body Studio

Create your own human: any gender, any age from 18 to 90, any build or ancestry. The body is a realistic
scanned-style human mesh, not boxes or blobs, and it breathes, blinks, moves and feels. Spin it a full 360°,
zoom into the face, then look inside: skeleton, organs, muscles, blood vessels and nerves, fitted to the body
you made.

Open `body-studio/index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari 16.4+), on desktop or
phone. It also opens from the game's title screen (**🧬 Body Studio**). There's nothing to install, and it
works straight from disk.

## What you can do

- **Shape the body.** Use the female↔male slider (any value in between), plus age, height, weight, muscle and
  proportions. There's an ancestry mix, breast size and firmness, and 13 body-type silhouettes. Over 40 sliders
  cover the torso, chest, hips, legs and arms.
- **Shape the face.** Choose a head shape, then adjust the eyes, brows, nose, mouth, jaw and chin, cheeks and
  ears with about 60 sliders.
- **Skin.** Set the tone and undertone, freckles, blush, shine, veins, lip colour and makeup. The skin uses
  subsurface-scattering shading and pore-level detail, and wrinkles deepen with age.
- **Hair.** Strand hair comes in many styles and colours, with length, volume and curl controls, greying with
  age, and physics. You can also set eyebrows, eyelashes, facial hair and body hair.
- **Eyes.** Pick from the iris colours or choose your own, with optional heterochromia.
- **Outfits.** Choose underwear, swimwear, sportswear, casual, a dress or formal wear, in your own colours.
- **Motion.** Idle breathing, blinking and glances, plus walking, running, dancing, waving and more. The
  person can look at the camera and talk.
- **Emotion.** Over a dozen emotions combine face, body language, breathing and blushing. Some animate, such as
  laughing and crying.
- **Inside.** Turn on the skin x-ray, muscles, skeleton, organs (the heart beats and the lungs breathe),
  vessels or nerves. Slice the body with the cross-section cut, then tap a structure to learn what it is.
- **Characters.** Load a preset or randomize, use undo and redo, save named characters in your browser,
  export or import them as JSON, and take screenshots.

**Controls:**

- Drag to spin the person 360°. On a phone, use one finger.
- Scroll or pinch to zoom.
- Right-drag or two fingers to pan.

**Keyboard shortcuts:**

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Space | Auto-spin |
| 1 to 5 | Camera presets |
| R | Randomize |
| Ctrl+Z | Undo |

## How it works

- **The human.** The body is MakeHuman's `hm08` base mesh, smoothed once with Catmull–Clark subdivision in
  the browser. Gender, age, muscle, weight, height, proportions, ancestry and the detail sliders blend 440 of
  MakeHuman's scanned morph targets, using the same weighting rules as MakeHuman.
- **Rig.** MakeHuman's 163-bone default skeleton is refit to every body shape, with 8-bone skinning. Facial
  expressions use MakeHuman's facial pose units.
- **Rendering.** three.js r159, with procedural textures and a studio light rig. No image files are loaded.
- **Code.** `js/core.js` (data, morphing, subdivision, skeleton fit), `js/human.js` (three.js character, pose
  API), `js/app.js` (renderer, orbit camera, module system), and one module per feature: `skin.js`,
  `motion.js`, `hair.js`, `clothing.js`, `anatomy.js` and `ui.js`. `dev/CONTRACT.md` documents the module API.
- **Data.** `assets/body-data.js` is generated by `tools/build_assets.py` from the MakeHuman and MPFB2
  repositories. See the script header for how to rebuild it.
